Title: Schrödinger Semigroups and Dynamical Potentials: Continuous Integral Kernels and Quantitative Parameter Regularity
Author: Georgy Dunaev (georgy.dunaev@uni-potsdam.de)
Institution: University of Potsdam, Institute of Mathematics (Institut für Mathematik, Universität Potsdam)
Scientific Advisor (Erstgutachter): Prof. Dr. Siegfried Beckus

1. Mathematical Programme & Advisor Tasks (Prof. Dr. Siegfried Beckus)

The thesis investigates self-adjoint Schrödinger operators H_x = \Delta + V_x on L^2(R^d) with nonnegative Laplacian \Delta = -\sum_{j=1}^d \partial_j^2 and bounded dynamical potentials V_x(g) = v(T(g, x)) (and negative-action convention \widetilde{V}_x(g) = v(T(-g, x))) generated by a continuous group action T: R^d x Z to Z on a compact metric space (Z, d_Z) and a Lipschitz or Hölder sampling function v: Z to R.

  • Part I.1 • Abstract Integral Kernel Criteria (Chapter 1, 01_preliminaries_and_simon_kernel_continuity.tex): Dunford-Pettis Theorem (Simon Theorem A.1.1 & Corollary A.1.2) and complete self-contained proof of Barry Simon (1982), Theorem A.1.3 characterizing bounded operators from L^1 to L^\infty with separately continuous integral kernels via weak-star continuous extensions from M(X) to C_b(X).
  • Part I.2 • Free Nonnegative Laplacian & Gaussian Heat Kernel (Chapter 2, 02_free_laplacian_and_jost_heat_kernel.tex): Complete proofs of Jürgen Jost (2013), Theorem 5.1.2 (parabolic maximum principle and Tikhonov-Gaussian uniqueness on R^d x [0, T]) and Jost Section 5.2 (explicit Gaussian heat kernel p_t(x - y) = (4 \pi t)^{-d/2} \exp(-\|x - y\|^2 / (4t)), Chapman-Kolmogorov semigroup law, unitary Fourier multiplier, and initial trace analysis on C_b(R^d) vs. BUC(R^d)).
  • Part I.3 • Kato-Rellich, Lie-Trotter Product Formula & Bounded-Potential Schrödinger Kernels (Chapter 3, 03_kato_rellich_trotter_and_cfks_bounded_potentials.tex): Self-adjointness on H^2(R^d) via Kato-Rellich; Duhamel variation-of-constants and Dyson series following Tosio Kato (1995), pp. 495 & 497-502; complete proof of the Lie-Trotter Product Formula following Joachim Weidmann (1980), Theorem 7.41; and simplified proof of Cycon, Froese, Kirsch, and Simon (CFKS 1987), Theorem 2.1 for bounded potentials V in L^\infty(R^d) establishing joint continuity, two-sided Gaussian bounds, hypercontractivity, and C^1 spatial regularity.
  • Part II.1-II.4 • Dynamical Potentials & Covariant Reduced Kernel (Chapter 4, 04_dynamical_potentials_and_covariant_kernel.tex): Unitary translation covariance U_a H_x U_a^{-1} = H_{T(a, x)}, derivation of the covariant reduced integral kernel k_t: Z x R^d to R satisfying (\exp(-t \widetilde{H}_x) \psi)(g) = \int_{R^d} k_t(T(-g, x), h - g) \psi(h) dh (and k_t(T(g, x), h - g) under the positive convention), and Gaussian/exponential decay in exponentially weighted Banach spaces C_a(R^d).
  • Part II.5 • Lipschitz & Hölder Continuity in the Z-Coordinate via Duhamel and Lie-Trotter (Chapter 5, 05_lipschitz_and_holder_continuity_in_z.tex): Two independent proofs that z maps to k_t(z, u) is quantitatively Lipschitz continuous in (Z, d_Z) (and C^{0, \alpha} Hölder continuous when v in C^{0, \alpha}(Z)): Method A via the pointwise Duhamel perturbation series and Gaussian bridge moments, and Method B via the n-step Lie-Trotter discrete path kernels and Riemann-sum convergence, plus localized L^2 operator bounds, the shear-torus counterexample, and 1/2-Hölder Hausdorff spectral continuity over invariant subsystems (specializing Beckus-Takase 2025).
