Author: Georgy Dunaev (2020)
Title: "Set-Theoretic Foundations of Mathematics" (AFP submission paper draft)

MEANING & PURPOSE:
This work formalizes results necessary for reasoning about mathematical foundations by means of the same foundations (metamathematics within set theory). Specifically, it formalizes the Transfinite Recursion Theorem, compatibility of sets of functions, and ordinal recursion in Isabelle/ZF. It directly precedes and informs the foundation design of the Obelisk Proof Assistant.

Key Theory & Manuscript Files

  • main.tex: LaTeX academic paper detailing the mathematical proof of the Transfinite Recursion Theorem and function compatibility.
  • recursion.thy: Machine-checked Isabelle/ZF theory proving the Recursion Theorem and defining arithmetic addition from set-theoretic primitives.
  • newzfc.thy: Foundational axioms and definitions for ZFC in Isabelle.
