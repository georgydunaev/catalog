1. Core Meta-Principles (Crystallized from 164 Operator Requests)

  • Principles Over Ad-Hoc Hacks: Prioritize creating and perfecting the Declaration of Principles over fast-and-bad ad-hoc implementations, especially in the kernel (typetheory.ML, newchecker/) and constructor namings. Never delete meaningful parts or historical commented-out ML blocks.
  • Anti-Bikeshedding Attention Allocation: Allocate >=80% of cognitive effort to deep mathematical, kernel, and architectural problems rather than letting trivial surface tweaks dominate attention.
  • Simplify Without Oversimplifying (Non-Contradiction Boundary): Unify genuinely isomorphic concepts (0-ary ops = constants; conglomerates = predicates on classes) while decoupling concepts whose conflation causes paradoxes: MKTD1 vs. MKTD2; Metalevel 0 ($\mathrm{Fm}_0$, guarded by is_object_level_fm0) vs. Metalevel +1/+2 (TpHolds, FFI, IFV); Semantic Class (Nonstored.t) vs. Symbol (Cp*/Tp*) vs. .Expre (CH*/HP*) vs. .Expression (C*/F*_<ArgTypes>_*_Expression) vs. Reflected Term; Binder (VarPos_Binder) vs. Occurrence; Dunaev pairs (A,B) vs. Kuratowski set pairs opairK(a,b); Semantic Completeness (3.73) vs. Syntactic Negation-Completeness (3.74); and the 4 arrows (—→ implication, → MKTD2 type arrow, ⟹ meta-constructor arrow, and relational shorthand f : A → B).
